# 6.0001 Introduction to Computer Science and Programming in Python

Course Number: 6.0001
Term: Fall 2016
Level: Undergraduate
Department: Electrical Engineering and Computer Science

Instructors: Dr. Ana Bell, Prof. Eric Grimson, Prof. John Guttag

## Course Meeting Times

> Lectures: 2 sessions / week, 1 hour / session
>
> Recitations: 1 sessions / week, 1 hour / session

## Course Description

"6.0001 Introduction to Computer Science and Programming in Python is intended for
students with little or no programming experience." The course uses Python 3.5 and is a
half-semester offering. Successful completion allows students to continue to 6.0002.

## Grading

> Grades will be roughly computed as follows:

| ACTIVITIES                                | PERCENTAGES |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Problem sets                              | 30%         |
| Completion of mandatory finger exercises  | 10%         |
| Midterm Quiz                              | 20%         |
| Final Quiz                                | 40%         |

## Problem Sets

> Each problem set will involve programming in Python. There will be 6 problem sets in the
> course. There will be two quizzes. All quizzes will be closed-book, though you will be
> allowed to bring one page of notes to the first quiz and two pages of notes to the second
> quiz.

Problem sets are graded out of 10 points.

> Submissions that do not run will receive at most 20% of the points.

## Late Days

> At the beginning of the term, students are given two late days that they can use on
> problem sets.
>
> Starting with Problem Set 1, additional late days can be accumulated for each assignment,
> one late day for each day the assignment is turned in ahead of the deadline. Up to three
> late days may be accumulated in this fashion in this course.

## Dropping / Rolling Over Problem Sets

Before the final quiz, students may drop up to 2 problem sets.

> If dropped, the percent that the problem sets are worth will be rolled into the final quiz
> score.

## Attendance

> A significant portion of the material for this course will presented only in lecture, so
> students are expected to regularly attend lectures.
>
> Recitation attendance is encouraged but not required.

## Assessment Summary

- 6 problem sets (programming in Python)
- 2 closed-book quizzes (notes permitted: 1 page for the midterm quiz, 2 pages for the final
  quiz)
- Mandatory finger exercises

Note: this syllabus states no calendar dates. Quizzes and problem sets are described only in
course-relative terms ("the first quiz", "Problem Set 1", "before the final quiz").
